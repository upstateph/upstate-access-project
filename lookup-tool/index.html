Upstate Access Project · Tier 2 pilot

Can you actually reach care from here?

Enter a Greenville County address to see how long it really takes to reach the nearest health center — on foot and by Greenlink transit.

It does not have to be where you live. A shelter, a library, a day center, or wherever you happen to be all work. Street and city is enough, no ZIP code needed.

🔒 No account, no login. We never store or log your address. To compute the result, it is sent to two public services — the US Census Geocoder (the address) and the OSRM routing network (coordinates only) — and nothing else. Only an anonymous, area-level count (service type, census tract, and travel times — nothing else) is kept here to build aggregate access statistics.

Walking and transit times are estimates (see the engine notes). FQHC locations: HRSA. Transit: Greenlink GTFS. This is a pilot — verify critical details directly with the provider.
