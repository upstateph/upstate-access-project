What this page measures — and what it doesn't

This page is about travel time to care. For every census tract it computes how long it takes to reach the nearest community health center by walking, driving, and Greenlink bus, using OSRM road-network routing and Greenlink's own published GTFS schedule. Transit is modelled from the real timetable — routes, stops, departure times and waits — not estimated from distance.

It is not built from pedestrian-crash data. This site also hosts a statewide pedestrian-safety tracker, and the two are separate analyses that happen to share a domain. No travel-time number on this page is derived from, weighted by, or adjusted for pedestrian deaths. An earlier attempt to connect the two was withdrawn after a null model refuted it, and the map that remains is descriptive context only.

Known limits, stated plainly. Travel time is an upper bound on real access: the model does not yet know which sites accept Medicaid or a given insurance, nor their opening hours, so a reachable clinic here may still be unusable in practice. Both are on the roadmap and both came from physician review.
