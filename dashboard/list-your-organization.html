List your organization

If you provide a health or social service in Greenville County and you would like people to be able to find you here, tell us and we will check the details with you.

What happens after you submit

Nothing appears on the site straight away, and that is deliberate. Every address here is confirmed by a person before it is published, because a wrong address means a wasted trip for someone who may not be able to afford the first one. Your submission joins the same queue a phone call would, and someone will contact you to confirm the details before anything is listed.

If you would rather not be listed at all, that is a completely reasonable position and you do not need to do anything. Some organizations serve their communities quietly on purpose.

If there is a check-in cut-off earlier than closing, please say so. It changes whether a journey is worth making and no public dataset records it.

Your name and email are used only to confirm these details and are never published. Nothing here is shared with any agency.
