How does this work?

You type a starting point. We route it on the real road network and against Greenlink's published timetable, and tell you how long each way actually takes. Nothing is stored and there is no account.

This site also hosts a separate statewide analysis of pedestrian safety and health equity. It is a different question, and no travel time here is derived from it. HRSA's health center locator lists where services are; this measures whether people can reach them.
