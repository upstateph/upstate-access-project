What this site is: the public analysis — where pedestrian danger and gaps in access to care overlap, and for whom. The companion address lookup (type an address → real walk, drive, bike and Greenlink bus time to the closest health center, pharmacy, or food assistance you can reach) is in private beta while its privacy hardening is finished. To find services directly today, use HRSA's health center locator — this project measures whether people can actually reach what those locators list.
