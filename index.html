How does this work?

You type a starting point. We route it on the real road network and against Greenlink's published timetable, and tell you how long each way actually takes. Nothing is stored, and there is no account.

Times are modeled, not observed. They are for comparing places, not for planning a single journey. The model sends you to whichever site is easiest to reach, so they are a floor on travel burden rather than the typical trip.

HRSA's health center locator lists where services are. This measures whether you can reach them.

Walking here is not a neutral option

Some of the walking times this tool reports are long, and South Carolina is among the worst states in the country for pedestrian deaths. If we are going to tell someone a place is a ninety-minute walk, that is worth saying alongside it.

See the statewide pedestrian-safety analysis → It is a separate piece of work. No travel time here is derived from, weighted by, or adjusted for pedestrian-death data.
