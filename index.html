How does this work?

You type a starting point. We use real roads and Greenlink's real bus schedule to work out how long each way takes. Bus times include the walk to the stop, the wait, the ride, and the walk at the end.

Other maps show you where clinics are. This one tells you whether you can actually get there, and it always shows the bus option, not just driving.

The fine print, in plain words

These times are estimates from public data, not measured trips. Use them to compare places, not to plan an exact journey. The tool points you to the easiest place to reach; if you already have a doctor somewhere else, your real trip may be longer. HRSA's health center locator is the official list of where health centers are.
